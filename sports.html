Malawi encourages girls to participate in football

Malawi is stepping up efforts to encourage girls to take part in football, with a call to establish a girls team. The move aims to discover young talent and give girls more opportunities to participate in sports.

According to a report published by Nyasa Times on 11 october 2026, Local Government Minister Ben Phiri called for schools to establish girls football teams, with support expected through the constituency development fund(CDF).

The initiative could help identify future players who may represent malawi at national and international level. It also offers girls an opportunity to development teamwork confidence and sporting skills. The football association of malawi has also been working to increase girls participation through grassroot football activities and pathways for young female players.

Malawi football teams battle for league positions

Football competition continues among clubs in malawi as teams fight for better positions in the national division league. Each match gives clubs an opportunity to collect points and improve their standung on the league. In matches played on 10 october 2026, mzuzu city hammers drew 0-0 with songwe board united recorded a 4-0 win over embangweni united. On 11 october 2026, FOMO FC defeated chilobwe united 1-0, while namitete zitha also won 1-0 against ndirande stars. These results showed how important every match can be in the competition. Songwe Board united remained at the top of the league table with 32 points after 14 matches in the standing published by the football association of malawi. Teams will need to maintain good performances as the competition continues. Supporters will be watching closely as clubs compete for points and aim to finish the season in the strong positions.

Malawi Launches 13,500 Bicycles to Help Keep Girls in School

The ministry of Education has launched a project providing 13,500 bicycles to help girls-travel to school more easily and reduce challenges that can affect their attendance

The initiative is aimed at supporting girls to remain in education by making it easier for them to travel to and from school.

The project is part of efforts to address challenges that can prevent some girls from attending school regulary

New Classrooms Help Bring Education Closer to Children in Lilongwe

Children in Chivuwo, Lilongwe, who once had to walk more than five kilometres to reach a full primary school are now benefiting from newly consturcted classrooms.

The project has helped improve the learning environment and increased access to education for children in the community.

The development is expected to make it easier for cildren in the area to attend school closer to their homes

Government Pledges to Improve Learning Environment at Blantyre Secondary School

The minister of Education, Bright Msaka, has pledged government support to improve the learning environment at Blantyre Secondary School.

The government says improving the learning environment is important for both students and teachers.
